Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 26 May 2026 | Effective for all users of GOVT ITI Balasore Digital Hub

Welcome to GOVT ITI Balasore Hub – your trusted student‑run digital resource for ITI education. This Privacy Policy explains in clear, plain language how we handle your information when you visit our website. We take your privacy seriously and operate with minimal data collection. Please read this entire document carefully. By using this website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

Quick Summary: We do NOT collect names, emails, phone numbers, Aadhaar, or any personal data unless you voluntarily fill our contact form. We use Google AdSense and RSS feeds which may set cookies for ads and analytics. No account registration, no comments, no database. This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages.

1. Information We Collect (Or Do Not Collect)

We DO NOT collect: Your name, email address (unless you voluntarily submit it via the Contact Us form), phone number, Aadhaar number, date of birth, financial information, or any other personally identifiable information (PII). We do not have any login system, user registration, comments section, or any feature that would require storing personal data on our servers.

Automatically collected (through third‑party services): When you browse our website, certain non‑personal information may be automatically collected by third‑party services embedded on our pages. This includes:

  • Google AdSense – collects IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and time spent on site. This data is anonymized and used to serve relevant advertisements.
  • RSS‑to‑JSON API – when we fetch live news from Google News, the RSS aggregator service may log your request IP temporarily for rate limiting and analytics.
  • GitHub Pages (Hosting) – as our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may collect standard web server logs including IP addresses and request timestamps for security and performance monitoring. Refer to GitHub’s Privacy Statement for details.

None of this automatically collected data is sold, shared, or used to identify you personally. It is used only for website analytics, security, and ad personalization (which you can opt out of).

2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Our website uses cookies primarily through the following services:

  • Google AdSense cookies (including DoubleClick DART cookie): These cookies are used to serve ads based on your interests and previous visits to our site or other websites. Google uses the DART cookie to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad Settings page.
  • Third‑party analytics (if any in future): Currently we do not use Google Analytics or similar. But if we add any analytics, it will be clearly disclosed here.

You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish. For more information, visit aboutcookies.org. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, some features of the website (like ad personalization) may not function properly without cookies.

Note: Our site does NOT use “fingerprinting”, “supercookies”, or any other covert tracking methods.

3. Third‑Party Services and Their Privacy Practices

We integrate only a few essential third‑party services to enhance functionality. Each has its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review:

  • Google AdSense – Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. AdSense may use data from your visits to this and other websites to serve personalized ads.
  • FormSubmit.co – Our contact form uses FormSubmit to forward messages to the developer’s email. Their privacy policy: https://formsubmit.co/privacy. They may temporarily log IP addresses to prevent spam.
  • RSS2JSON API (rss2json.com) – Used to fetch live news. Their policy: https://rss2json.com/privacy.html.
  • GitHub Pages (Hosting) – Privacy policy: GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts & Font Awesome – These services may collect IP addresses and browser data as per their policies.

We do not have control over the data collected by these third parties. This Privacy Policy applies only to our website. When you leave our site via external links (e.g., NCVT MIS, SAMS Odisha, NSP), you are subject to the privacy policies of those external websites.

4. Contact Form – What Happens to Your Data?

Our Contact Us page contains a simple form that asks for your name, email, and message. When you submit this form:

  • The data is sent directly to FormSubmit.co, which then forwards it to the developer’s email (avinandanasahani777@gmail.com).
  • We do NOT store this data in any database or on our server.
  • We use your name and email only to reply to your query. Your email will not be added to any newsletter or marketing list.
  • We may keep the email correspondence for up to 90 days for reference, after which it is deleted.
  • Never share sensitive personal information (Aadhaar, bank details, passwords) via the contact form.

If you do not want your data to be processed by FormSubmit, please do not use the contact form. Instead, you can reach out physically at the institute campus to the COPA department.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any information you submit (though we collect almost none). Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which uses HTTPS encryption to secure data transmission between your browser and the server. We do not run any databases, so there is no risk of database breach. The contact form messages are transmitted via HTTPS and then stored temporarily in the developer’s email inbox, which is password‑protected and two‑factor authentication enabled.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. By using our website, you acknowledge that you provide your information at your own risk.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Indian Laws)

Our website is intended for ITI trainees, who are typically aged 16 years or older. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information via the contact form, please contact us immediately (using the contact page) and we will delete that information promptly.

In compliance with the Indian Information Technology (Reasonable Security and Procedures) Rules, 2011, we do not require any user to disclose sensitive personal data for accessing the website.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR (For European Visitors)

Although we are based in India, we respect the rights of all users. If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). These include:

  • Right to access – You can request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification – You can ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”) – You can ask us to delete your personal data.
  • Right to restrict processing – You can object to certain uses of your data.
  • Right to data portability – You can request a machine‑readable copy of your data.

Because we collect almost no personal data, exercising these rights will typically result in us confirming that we have no data about you. For any request, please use our contact page. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Your Rights Under the Indian IT Act (2000) and SPDI Rules

Under Indian law, “sensitive personal data or information” (SPDI) includes passwords, financial information, physical/mental health, sexual orientation, biometric data, etc. We never collect any SPDI. Nevertheless, we provide you the following rights:

  • You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • You may request correction of your data (if we had any).
  • You may lodge a complaint with the relevant authorities (e.g., Ministry of Electronics & IT).

For grievances related to data handling, please contact our Grievance Officer (the lead developer) via the Contact Us page.

9. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information collected only for the following purposes:

  • To display relevant advertisements (via Google AdSense) that help keep this student project sustainable.
  • To reply to your queries when you contact us.
  • To improve our website content based on aggregate usage patterns (via IP anonymized logs).
  • To ensure security and prevent spam or malicious activities.

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your information with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes.

10. Data Retention

Since we do not maintain a database, the only data that may persist are:

  • Email correspondence (from contact form) – retained for up to 90 days, then deleted unless needed for ongoing issue resolution.
  • Server logs (GitHub Pages) – retained by GitHub for a limited period as per their policy.
  • Cookie data – stored on your device and can be cleared by you at any time.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated “Effective Date”. Major changes will be announced via a notice on our homepage (e.g., in the marquee or a banner). Your continued use of the website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the new Privacy Policy.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. The current version is always the one accessible via the “Privacy Policy” link in the footer and navigation.
